<h1 class="page-header">
  Adoption Procedure
</h1>

1. Please browse adoptadog.sg and <b>note</b> down the name and contact details of dogs you are interested in.<br><br>
2. <b>SMS or email</b> the welfare group to ask about the dog and their adoption procedure. <br><br>
3. You will be requested to fill in an adoption <b>form</b> about your personal details, experience with dogs etc.<br><br>
4. Next, you can arrange to meet the dog at agreed date and place. You should <b>assess</b> whether the dog is suitable for you and your family.<br><br>
5. Take a few days to <b>think</b> about it and discuss with your family. If you wish to back out, please politely inform the welfare group.<br><br>
6. If you wish to <b>adopt</b>, you will sign an adoption form and bring the dog home. <br><br>
7. If you need <b>assistance</b> with the dog in terms of toilet training, discipline, food to eat, call the welfare group who be will be glad to help you.<br><br>
