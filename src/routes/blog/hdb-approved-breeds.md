<h1 class="page-header">
  HDB Approved Breeds
</h1>

If you live in an HDB flat and plan to adopt, start with the official HDB pet guidelines:<br>
<a href='https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/keeping-pets' target='_blank'>https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/keeping-pets</a><br><br>

Here are the key points most adopters ask about:<br><br>

1. You can keep <b>1 dog</b> from HDB's approved breed list in each residential unit.<br>
2. HDB also allows <b>Project ADORE</b> adoptions for local mixed-breed dogs (Singapore Specials) up to 55cm in height, subject to the scheme requirements.<br>
3. From 1 Sep 2024, cat rules were updated, but dog owners must still comply with HDB and AVS licensing requirements.<br><br>

The approved breed list is maintained by HDB and may be updated over time. For the latest information, always refer to the official page above instead of relying on screenshots or reposted lists.<br><br>

If you are adopting through a welfare group, confirm these details before bringing your dog home:<br><br>

1. Whether your dog's breed (or cross) is covered under current HDB rules<br>
2. Whether your dog's size and profile fit Project ADORE conditions, if applicable<br>
3. What licensing, microchipping, sterilisation, and training steps are required by the adoption partner and authorities<br><br>

When in doubt, check directly with the welfare group and verify using official HDB and AVS sources.
