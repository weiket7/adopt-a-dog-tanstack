<h1 class="page-header">
    Project Adore
  </h1>

In May 2012, SPCA and ASD spearheaded <a href='https://www.mnd.gov.sg/our-work/engaging-our-communities/project-adore'>Project ADORE</a> (Project on Adoption and Rehoming of dogs), allowing local mixed-breed dogs under 55cm to be allowed in HDB flats. Under Project ADORE, the adopter needs to:<br>
<br>

1. Sign the adoption agreement with SPCA or ASD, and pay the adoption fee<br>
2. Microchip, vaccinate, sterilise and license the dog<br>
3. Undergo compulsory Basic Obedience Training<br>
4. Sign Code of Responsible Behavior (CORB)<br>
   <br>

Every year, an average of about 250 dogs are rehomed under Project ADORE.
